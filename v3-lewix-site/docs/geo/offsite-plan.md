# Off-site plan: lewix.ai

Measured 2026-10-09: no Common Crawl captures in the last three indexes, no Wayback history. For a
site this new the limiting factor is that few places link to it, so engines have little reason to
retrieve it and future models will not have seen it.

In order of effort to value:

1. **Profiles with the exact entity facts** (legal name, SSM 202601027756, Kuala Lumpur, lewix.ai,
   hello@lewix.ai): Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, LinkedIn company
   page, Facebook page. Same name spelling everywhere, each linking to lewix.ai.
2. **Link the products back.** SmoothSail, PlanForge and WireUp are live and public; each can carry
   a "Built by Lewix AI" link to lewix.ai. Real, relevant links from sites you own.
3. **Founder profiles.** Lewis, Noel and Fakhrul's LinkedIn experience entries pointing at
   lewix.ai with the same company name.
4. **Malaysian business and developer directories** that rank for "software house Malaysia":
   check which domains the engines cite once the first measurement wave runs, then list there.
   Do not pay for placements in self-ranked "best" lists.
5. **One piece of original data** worth citing, e.g. what Malaysian SMEs run their operations
   on before they replace it (spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, SQL Account), drawn from discovery
   calls, anonymised. Publish it on lewix.ai with method and sample size.
6. **Reviews** from clients who agree to be named on a review platform, never gated, never
   written for them. If no client can be named, skip it: client confidentiality comes first.

Not doing: Wikipedia or Wikidata (not notable yet), Reddit or forum seeding, link buying.
